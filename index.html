How to run it

  • Progression: when you hit the top of the rep range on every set, add weight next session (≈2.5 kg / 5 lb upper, 5 kg / 10 lb lower).
  • Effort: finish most sets 1–2 reps shy of failure. Last set of isolation work can go to failure.
  • Warm-up: 5 min light cardio, then 2–3 ramp-up sets on the first compound lift.
  • Rest: 2–3 min on big compounds, 60–90 s on accessories. Use the timer below.
